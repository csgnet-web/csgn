# CSGN Marketing Audit & Campaign Blueprint

> An agency-grade audit — brand, audience, positioning, creative, channels,
> funnel, and a 90-day calendar — for CSGN. Written the way a modern shop would
> pitch it: strategy first, then the Big Idea, then the work. Companion to
> [`docs/master-plan.md`](./master-plan.md).

---

## 0. The brutally honest situation

- **Product:** top-1% for the category. **Awareness:** ~zero. **Token cap:** ~$4k.
- The gap is not creative quality — it's that **no one is in the room.** Every
  dollar and hour of marketing must buy *attention and holders*, not polish.
- Constraint: essentially a solo operator, near-zero budget. So the plan is
  **earned + borrowed + owned**, not paid. Guerrilla, not Super Bowl.

The good news: a $4k cap and a broadcast-grade product is a *coiled spring*. You
don't need millions of people. You need the **right few thousand degens** to
adopt "the ESPN of crypto" as an identity.

## 1. Brand strategy

### 1.1 The Big Idea
> **"Crypto has a TV channel now."**

Everything ladders to that. Not "a streaming site," not "a token" — a *channel*.
Channels have shows, a ticker, appointment times, personalities, and a remote.
CSGN's unfair advantage is that it *looks and behaves like a network* in a
category of webcams. Lead with the format; the token rides in on it.

### 1.2 Positioning statement
*For crypto-native degens who live in markets all day, CSGN is the 24/7 TV network
where watching crypto and trading it are the same act — because unlike a Discord,
a chart, or a chaotic pump.fun stream, CSGN is broadcast-grade and coin-operated:
you pay to put your coin on air and you hold the token to run the channel.*

### 1.3 Brand character
Confident, fast, a little chaotic-good. **Bloomberg's authority meets a sports
bar's energy meets a degen's sense of humor.** It should feel like the news
anchor is *in* on the joke but the graphics are dead serious.

### 1.4 The line
**"The ESPN of crypto. Coin-operated."** (Tagline.)
Support lines: *"Hold the token. Hold the remote."* · *"Pay a coin, play a coin."*
· *"Watch it happen live."*

## 2. Audience — who we're actually talking to

Three concentric rings. Win the core first; the others follow.

### Ring 1 — The Degens (core, ~80% of effort)
Solana-native traders on X all day, in coin Telegrams, allergic to "marketing."
- **Want:** alpha, status, entertainment, a place their coin can get seen.
- **Hook:** the Coin Jukebox ("get your coin on TV") + the meme-100 power ranking.
- **Where:** X (CT), coin Telegrams, pump.fun, Dexscreener.

### Ring 2 — Coin Communities & KOLs (distribution multipliers)
Memecoin founders/mods and mid-tier CT accounts who need attention for their bag.
- **Want:** eyeballs on their coin; a co-sign; a stage.
- **Hook:** spotlights, the Live Coin Battle, sponsoring a dynasty team. They
  market CSGN to *their* holders to win — borrowed audience by design.

### Ring 3 — Streamers & Sports/Gaming Fans (retention)
CFB dynasty players, watch-party crowds, small streamers.
- **Want:** to earn (fee share), to play, to belong.
- **Hook:** creator-fee revenue share; vote-to-steer the dynasty; the game leagues.

**Persona cards (shorthand):**
- *"Ansem-lite"* — 20k-follower CT trader. Give him a segment, he brings his
  audience.
- *"Mod Mike"* — runs a 5k-holder coin TG. Sell him a spotlight; he raids for you.
- *"Dynasty Dan"* — streams CFB to 40 viewers. Give him the fee share + a league.

## 4. The creative — campaign concepts

Three launch campaigns, each a repeatable format, not a one-off.

### Campaign A — "Get Your Coin On TV" (the acquisition engine)
The Coin Jukebox as a meme. Short vertical clips: a coin gets *played* on the CSGN
ticker, the spotlight rises, the ticker reads "512 backers · burned." CTA: *"Pay a
coin, play a coin — csgn.fun."* Every clip is an ad *and* proof the product works.
Coin communities will make these for you if you give them the moment.

### Campaign B — "Live Coin Battle" (the appointment)
Two communities' coins fight for the night's spotlight, decided by on-chain volume
+ holder votes, narrated live. Weekly. Trailer'd on X, clipped after. This is the
tentpole — it manufactures a reason to show up at a time, and both fanbases market
it for you. **The single highest-leverage marketing act available.**

### Campaign C — "Hold the Remote" (the token narrative)
A tight explainer series: *"$CSGN isn't a memecoin. It's the remote control for
crypto TV."* Show a holder voting the meme-100 from their phone and the ticker
changing on the broadcast. The magic trick: **your phone changes what's on TV.**
End every one with the burn line: *"…and every feature burns it."*

**Creative principles:**
- **Show the phone-changes-the-TV trick constantly.** It's the whole product in
  three seconds.
- **The ticker is the brand.** Put it in every asset; it's instantly "a network."
- **Make the coin the hero, not CSGN.** Degens share things that make *their bag*
  look important.
- **Native, not polished-ad.** CT smells an ad and scrolls. Shoot like a shitpost
  with broadcast graphics.

## 5. Channel strategy (earned · borrowed · owned)

- **X (primary):** 3–5 posts/day — clips, the daily meme-100 #1, battle trailers,
  the burn count, replies into big CT threads. Reply-guy your way into reach; you
  have the best visual asset in any thread.
- **Coin Telegrams (borrowed):** a repeatable "your coin can be on TV tonight"
  outreach to mods. Each yes = a raid + a spotlight clip.
- **pump.fun / Dexscreener (context):** be where coins are born; offer the newest
  ones a spotlight.
- **The broadcast itself (owned):** the 24/7 feed is a 24/7 ad. Bake CTAs into the
  interstitials (already built): "vote at csgn.fun," "play your coin."
- **YouTube/TikTok (repurpose):** re-cut the best clips; long-tail discovery the
  24/7 VOD backbone feeds for free.

**Do NOT** buy generic crypto ads or engagement pods. Waste.

## 6. The content engine (how one person feeds all this)

The 24/7 board is a **clip factory** — the marketing is mostly *harvesting*, not
creating:
1. **Capture** the best 30–60s of each night (a battle finish, a spotlight rise, a
   coin 3x'ing on the ticker).
2. **Caption** with the coin's ticker + the format line.
3. **Post** to X daily; tag the coin community; let them re-share.
4. **Recut** weekly into a YouTube/TikTok highlight.

One founder can run this in ~30 min/day *if* it's a habit, not a project. This is
the 80/20: harvesting clips is the cheapest, highest-ROI marketing available.

## 7. Funnel & metrics (manage to these, not price)

| Stage | Metric | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | impressions / reshares on X | X analytics |
| Interest | concurrent viewers, /watch visits | X Media Studio, site |
| Wallet | wallet connects | app |
| **Hold** | **holders** | on-chain |
| **Act** | **holders-who-acted this week** (votes, spotlights, submissions) | the built fan-action counter |
| Advocate | coin communities activated | CRM/spreadsheet |

**North Star: holders-who-acted per week.** It's the one number that means the
audience is becoming the business. Market cap is the scoreboard, not the game.

## 8. 90-day launch calendar

**Weeks 1–2 — Foundation**
- Rewrite homepage + X bio + pinned post to "Crypto has a TV channel now / the
  remote control for crypto TV, and every feature burns it."
- Dry-run the jukebox on mainnet; ship the on-air burn counter.
- Start the daily clip habit. Soft-launch "Get Your Coin On TV."

**Weeks 3–4 — First ignition**
- Run **Live Coin Battle #1** with two seeded coin communities. Trailer it 48h out;
  clip it hard after.
- Outreach to 10 coin mods with the spotlight offer.

**Month 2 — Rhythm**
- Weekly Live Coin Battle becomes appointment TV. "Hold the Remote" explainer
  series (3 clips). Launch the CFB 27 Dynasty with recruiting-by-vote.
- First KOL segment (borrowed audience).

**Month 3 — Compounding**
- 10+ communities cycling through spotlights/battles. Publish the weekly holders-
  who-acted + burn numbers as a recurring "State of the Network" post.
- Pilot creator coins / dynasty sponsorships.
