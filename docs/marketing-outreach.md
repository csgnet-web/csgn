# CSGN — outreach and creative

Everything a campaign needs, written to be used rather than admired: the
positioning, the copy, the scripts, the calendar, and the rules about what we
are not allowed to say.

Read [product-process.md](product-process.md) first. **Do not spend a dollar or
send a cold DM before Gate 1 is green and Gate 2 is under way** — buying
attention for a dark network is the most expensive mistake available here.

---

## 1. Positioning

### The strategic premise

Every streaming platform monetises the *audience* — ads, subs, tips — and pays
the creator a cut of what the platform collected. CSGN monetises the *market the
audience creates* and pays the creator out of it directly, per hour, on-chain.

That is one genuinely new sentence in a category full of recycled ones, and it
is checkable, which is the only thing that survives contact with a crypto
audience.

### The line

> **You're on. The fees are yours.**

Alternates, by context:

| Context | Line |
|---|---|
| Streamer recruiting | **Keep your channel. Keep your show. Get paid from the tape.** |
| Viewer / token | **Watch the show. Watch the fees. Same screen.** |
| Investor / partner | **The first network where the creator is paid from the market their attention makes.** |
| One-liner, anywhere | **Streaming, paid by the order book.** |

### The three-sentence talk track — memorise this

1. CSGN is a 24/7 crypto network — streamers claim an hour and go live on their
   own Twitch channel.
2. While they're on air, the trading fees the token generates are split, and the
   streamer takes 30% of the creator fee for their hour.
3. It's calculated per slot from live market data and settled on Solana, so it's
   not a promise — it's a transaction you can look up.

### The proof stack, in the order to deploy it

1. **A payout transaction hash.** One screenshot of SOL landing in a streamer's
   wallet beats every word in this document. Produce this before anything else.
2. **The live fee readout on air.** A number going up on screen during a
   broadcast is the mechanism explaining itself.
3. **The open-source repository.** MIT licensed, 586 tests, documented well
   enough to fork. For technical evaluators this is disproportionately
   persuasive, and almost nobody in this category can show it.
4. **The schedule.** A grid with names in it is the proof that other people
   already said yes.

### What we do not say

Non-negotiable, and not only for legal reasons — the on-chain record means
every exaggeration is publicly falsifiable within a day.

- ❌ No earnings figures that are not computed from the live fee table. **Never
  imply a monthly income.** Say "your hour paid $14 last Tuesday" if that's what
  it paid.
- ❌ No "investment", "returns", "yield", "passive income", or price prediction
  of any kind, ever, in any asset.
- ❌ No claim of partnership, endorsement or affiliation with Twitch, X,
  Phantom, Solana or pump.fun. We integrate with them; we are not with them.
- ❌ No fabricated viewer counts, streamer counts or testimonials.
- ✅ Do say: small-but-real, early, growing, verifiable, "here's the hash".

The honest frame is also the strong one: *"It's a small number today and I'll
show you exactly how it's calculated"* outperforms a big vague number with this
audience, every time.

## 2. Audiences, and the one thing each needs to hear

| # | Audience | Where they are | The message | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Crypto-native small streamers** (10–200 CCV, already stream charts/pump.fun/trading) | X crypto timeline, Solana Discords, pump.fun livestreams | "You're already doing this for free. Claim an hour and get paid from the tape." | Claim one hour this week |
| 2 | **Crypto traders who don't stream** | X, DexScreener, Telegram | "There's a market show running 24/7 with the fee meter on screen." | Watch one hour |
| 3 | **$CSGN holders and speculators** | Token channels | "Every hour aired is fees generated and a payout published." | Hold, amplify the receipts |
| 4 | **Investors / operators** | Warm intros, X DMs | "The creator is paid from the market their attention creates, and the payout is on-chain." | 20 minutes |
| 5 | **Non-crypto Twitch streamers** | Twitch, Discord | — | **Not addressable today.** See below. |

**On audience 5 and the Phantom question.** The product requires a Phantom
wallet with on-chain history to create an account, which self-selects for
crypto-native users. That is a legitimate choice — the payout goes to a wallet,
so at some point a wallet must exist — but it means **audience 5 cannot be
marketed to at all right now**, and any campaign aimed at general Twitch
streamers will burn its budget on people who bounce at the wallet step.

So the media plan below targets audiences 1–4 exclusively. That is the right
call for the first campaign: those people already have Phantom, the whole
mechanism is legible to them without explanation, and they are the ones who
generate the trading volume the payout is made of. Widening to audience 5 is a
*product* decision (`product-process.md` §4.4, option b — claim with Twitch,
attach the wallet at payout), not a creative one, and it should not be attempted
in the same week as a campaign.

## 3. The creative platform: **"THE TAPE PAYS."**

One idea, executed everywhere: *the market is already paying someone — it should
be paying the person on screen.*

Every asset shows two things in one frame: **a face talking** and **a number
moving**. That is the entire visual system, and it is the mechanism, so it
cannot be mistaken for a slogan.

**Visual rules.** CSGN red on near-black. Broadcast furniture — lower third,
ticker, LIVE bug — because it should read as a network, not a dapp. The fee
counter is always visible, always real, never mocked up. Mono type for numbers,
display type for names. No stock photography, no 3D coins, no rockets.

**Audio/motion rule.** The first 1.5 seconds of any video is the number moving.
Not the logo.

### 4.4 X posts — the founder account (the growth engine)

People follow people. This account carries the opinion and the receipts; the
project account carries the evidence. Build-in-public posts are the cheapest
distribution available and technical founders under-post their own work by an
order of magnitude.

> Spent today deleting our sign-up form.
> Email, password, confirm password, and a Twitch hop that literally cannot
> complete inside Phantom's in-app browser — Google blocks OAuth in webviews,
> so "Continue with Google" returns "Something went wrong" by design.
> It's three taps now. One signature. Here's what it looks like: 🎥

> Uncomfortable number: at a $2M market cap you need $68k/day of volume to make
> $10k/month in fees. At $100M you need $953k/day for the *same money*, because
> the fee rate collapses from 0.70% to 0.05%.
> The market-cap goal and the income goal fight each other. Most projects never
> run this math.

> A streamer promised $200 who receives $12 never comes back — and on-chain,
> you can't quietly round up.
> So we quote the real number. Last night's hour paid {Z} SOL. Small. Real.
> Growing. {tx}

### 4.5 Paid ad copy — X Ads

Only X. Google and Meta both restrict crypto advertising heavily and neither
will reliably serve this creative; the audience is on X anyway.

**Set A — streamer acquisition** *(target: followers of Solana/pump.fun/trading
creators; interests: Twitch, streaming, Solana)*

| | Primary text | Headline | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | You already stream charts for free. On CSGN you claim an hour, stream to your own Twitch, and take 30% of the trading fees your hour generates. Paid in SOL. | Claim an hour | Sign up |
| A2 | Sign-up is one wallet signature. No email, no password, no application. Claim an hour tonight and the fee meter runs while you talk. | Three taps to on air | Sign up |
| A3 | Nobody's on air at 11pm ET tonight. That's an opening — and the fees from that hour go to whoever takes it. | The stage is open | See open hours |

**Set B — viewer / awareness** *(target: $CSGN holders, Solana traders, crypto X)*

| | Primary text | Headline | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | A 24/7 crypto network where the streamer's earnings are on screen, live, and settled on-chain. | Watch the tape pay | Watch live |
| B2 | Every hour on CSGN generates fees. 30% goes to whoever is on air. Here's last night's transaction. | Receipts, hourly | Watch live |

**Test discipline.** One variable at a time. Headline first (A1 vs A2 vs A3,
same creative, same audience, 72 hours, ~$50 each). Then creative on the winner.
Then audience. Kill anything below half the leader's cost-per-signup at the
72-hour mark. **The metric is accounts created, not clicks** — clicks are free
to buy and mean nothing here.

**Budget shape for a first test:** $500 over two weeks. $300 to Set A, $150 to
Set B, $100 held back to boost whichever organic post outperforms — which it
usually will, because a payout receipt is better creative than anything in this
section.

## 5. Outreach scripts

### 5.1 Cold DM — a crypto streamer *(the workhorse)*

Rules: under 90 words, no link in the first message unless they're clearly
warm, one specific detail proving you watched them, and an ask that costs them
nothing.

> Hey — caught your {specific thing, e.g. "pump.fun scanner stream last
> Thursday"}. Genuinely good.
>
> Quick thing: I run CSGN, a 24/7 crypto network. You claim an hour, stream to
> **your own** Twitch exactly like you already do, and you take 30% of the
> $CSGN trading fees generated during your hour, paid in SOL.
>
> It's small money today — last night's hour paid {Z} SOL, here's the tx — but
> it costs you nothing, since you were streaming anyway.
>
> Want an hour this week? Sign-up is one wallet signature.

**Follow-up, once, after 4 days:**

> No worries if it's not for you — one thing in case it's useful: {tx link} is
> last night's payout. Door's open whenever.

Then stop. A third message costs more than it earns.

### 5.2 Warm DM — someone who has engaged

> You commented on the fee-split post — want to try it? I'll hold you an hour
> this week, prime time. You stream your normal show on your channel, I'll run
> the payout on air afterwards so you can see the number land.

### 5.3 Guest ask — the easiest yes there is

> Come on the show Thursday for 30 minutes to talk {their topic}? No pitch, no
> obligation. Most people who guest end up claiming an hour later, but that's
> not the ask.

Every guest is a candidate streamer, and guesting is a far smaller ask than
"claim an hour on my platform."

### 5.4 Investor / partner outreach

Subject: **The creator is paid from the market their attention creates**

> {Name} —
>
> CSGN is a 24/7 crypto streaming network on Solana. The mechanism is one
> sentence: the streamer on air takes 30% of the trading fees the token
> generates during their hour, calculated from live market data and settled
> on-chain.
>
> Three things you can check without me:
> • The payout: {tx hash}
> • The code: github.com/csgnet-web/csgn — MIT, 586 tests, the fee tiers are in
>   `_shared/feeCalc.ts`
> • The network, live: csgn.fun
>
> Where it stands, plainly: the build is ahead of the distribution. {N}
> streamers, {M} hours aired, ${F} paid out. I'm looking for {the ask}.
>
> 20 minutes this week?

Note what this does not do: no deck attached, no market-size slide, no
hockey-stick. For this audience the repository and the transaction hash *are*
the deck, and leading with them signals a category of founder.

### 5.5 The elevator version — 30 seconds, out loud

> You know how Twitch pays streamers out of ads and subs? CSGN pays them out of
> the trading fees their own audience creates. You claim an hour, you stream on
> your own channel, and 30% of the fees from that hour go to your wallet — on
> Solana, so you can check it.
>
> *(hand them the phone)* — that's the sign-up. Tap that, approve in Phantom,
> pick a name. You've got an account. Three taps.

The demo *is* the pitch. Three taps and fifteen seconds is the only reason this
paragraph works, which is why the sign-up rebuild came before the campaign.

## 7. The first 30 days

Runs concurrently with Gates 2 and 3 in [product-process.md](product-process.md)
§5. Nothing here starts before Gate 1 is green.

**Week 1 — receipts exist**
Stream 3 sessions on the fixed schedule. Run one payout on air and publish the
hash. Pin the mechanism thread. Post the sign-up-rebuild video (§4.4). Zero
paid, zero cold DMs.

**Week 2 — the list**
Keep streaming 3 sessions. Build a list of 50 crypto streamers, by hand, with a
specific note on each. Send 10 cold DMs (§5.1). Ask 3 guests. Start the £/$500
X Ads test on Set A only.

**Week 3 — the first outside streamer**
Land 2–3 outside streamers into prime-time hours. Feature each one hard: on-air
mention, clip, payout receipt on @CSGNet. Send 20 more DMs, now with a second
streamer's payout to quote. Add Set B to the ad test.

**Week 4 — make the schedule look inhabited**
Target 10 booked hours across the week. Publish the week's grid as a single
image on Sunday — the schedule graphic is the best recruiting asset the project
will have, because it proves other people said yes. Review the six metrics in
`product-process.md` §6 and write them down.

**Daily, throughout, non-negotiable:** 3–5 founder posts, mostly replies under
larger accounts in the niche. It is the highest-leverage growth mechanic on X
and it is unglamorous enough that most people won't do it consistently.

## 8. Creative briefs

**1 · The payout clip** *(15s, vertical)* — **build this first**
Screen recording of the payout running on stream, cutting to the transaction on
a block explorer, cutting to the streamer's reaction. No music bed under the
reaction. End card: `csgn.fun` + "the stage is open tonight". *This asset is
worth more than the entire ad budget.*

**2 · The three-tap sign-up** *(12s, vertical, screen recording)*
A real phone, in Phantom's browser. Tap Join → approve → Create account. On-screen
counter: "1 … 2 … 3". End card: "That's the whole sign-up." Nothing staged, no
speed-ramping — the point is that it is genuinely this short.

**3 · The fee meter** *(6s, loop, silent)*
Tight crop of the live earnings readout ticking up beside the LIVE bug. Caption:
"this number is the streamer's." Autoplay-safe, works as a reply asset forever.

**4 · The weekly schedule card** *(static, 1200×675)*
The week's grid, names in the booked hours, "OPEN" in the empty ones. Posted
every Sunday. Recruiting and social proof in one image.

**5 · OG / share image** *(1200×630)*
CSGN mark, "You're on. The fees are yours.", the live LIVE/OFFLINE state if it
can be generated dynamically. Currently `public/csgn-og.png` — verify it renders
correctly in the X card validator before any campaign, because every link posted
for the next month depends on it.
