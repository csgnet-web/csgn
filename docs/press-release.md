## CSGN launches a 24-hour crypto television channel that anyone can get on — and pays the people who fill it

**A network with no upload button, no feed, and no algorithm. You paste a link
to something you already made, and it goes on television.**

---

**[CITY] — [DATE]** — CSGN (Crypto Sports & Gaming Network) today launched a
continuously broadcasting television channel for crypto, assembled entirely from
content its own members supply. The channel runs 24 hours a day on Solana-native
infrastructure and has no editorial staff, no submission window, and no
gatekeeper deciding who deserves an audience.

There are two ways onto it, and both are close to free.

**Post a clip.** A member pastes a link to a video they already published on
YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. Nothing is uploaded — the platform that already
hosts the video keeps hosting it. A human reviews it, and then it airs between
live streams. The member does not have to be there when it does.

**Connect your Twitch, then forget about it.** A streamer links their channel
once and grants permission to be carried. CSGN checks every consenting channel
about once a minute; when a streamer goes live, the network can put them on air
in a single click. There is no time slot to book, no software to install, and
nothing to remember. They stream the way they already stream, and CSGN carries
them when it makes sense.

### The part that has not been done before

How much of the channel's open air a member is entitled to is **one to one with
the $CSGN they hold.** Hold one percent of the supply, and one percent of the
day is yours.

That number is not a promise, a points balance, or a reward that vests. It is
computed from the member's wallet balance, read live from the Solana chain,
every time they look at it — and it is shown alongside its own working: the
balance, the share of supply, and the wallet it came from. A member can check
every input against a block explorer.

"Every platform in this space has a token that does something vague and a
product that would work identically without it," said [SPOKESPERSON], [TITLE] at
CSGN. "We wanted the token to do exactly one thing, and for that thing to be
measurable to the second. Your bag buys airtime on a real channel. That's it.
You can hold up your phone and watch the thing you own turn into something on
screen."

Holding is not staking. Nothing is burned, locked, escrowed or deposited. A
member's weight is simply what is in their wallet at that moment; sell tomorrow
and it goes with them.

### Streamers are paid for minutes actually broadcast

Streamers earn a share of $CSGN trading fees for the minutes CSGN actually
carried them — not for being live on their own channel, and not for reserving a
block they did not show up to. The network samples channels once a minute and
keeps two separate counters: minutes live, and minutes on air. Only the second
one pays.

That distinction is deliberate. A network that pays for availability rather than
delivery is a network that eventually pays for nothing.

### What is on when nobody is live

The clip reel. This is not a placeholder — it is the product's spine. A channel
that goes dark 90% of the day cannot be linked, embedded, or shared, and cannot
recruit the streamers that would fill it. A channel that always has something on
can do all three.

CSGN's operator board raises the decision automatically: it watches every
connected channel, and when a live stream clears an audience threshold it
suggests putting them on. Below that threshold, the reel keeps the air —
because a stream with four viewers is worse television than a curated reel, and
interrupting the reel for it would cost holders the airtime they paid for.

### Also launching

- **The Meme 100** — a live ranking of Solana memecoins assembled from on-chain
  trading rather than from a hand-typed list, scored 0–100 on a published
  formula that weights momentum over size. Holders move the ranking with their
  $CSGN, and the whole breakdown is one tap away on every row.
- **The Coin Jukebox** — an open auction for the broadcast's coin spotlight,
  paid in $CSGN to a public treasury. The highest live bid holds the spotlight
  for twelve hours. Every paid placement is labelled as paid on screen.
- **A published treasury.** Everything the network takes in goes to one wallet
  under published rules, and the balance is on the site.

### Open source

The entire network — the scheduler, the payout logic, the broadcast graphics,
the on-chain verification — is MIT licensed at
[github.com/csgnet-web/csgn](https://github.com/csgnet-web/csgn). Anyone can
fork it, point the wallets at their own, and run a competing channel.

"We're not the only network that could exist," said [SPOKESPERSON]. "We'd rather
be the first one that published how it works."
