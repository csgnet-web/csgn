> **Status: operating document.** The founder's account, run as a system rather than as a
> mood. Sits under [`plan.md`](plan.md), which owns the quarter and the targets; this owns
> the daily execution.
>
> **Evidence note.** X returns 403 to unauthenticated requests, so nothing here is derived
> from live account data. The follower numbers are the founder's own. Where this document
> would otherwise need a list of specific accounts, it gives a **procedure for building
> one** instead — a fabricated handle is worse than no handle.

---

## 0. The arithmetic, stated first

**+6,450 followers in 90 days is ~72 a day.** That number should shape every decision in
this document, because it rules things out:

- Replies alone will not do it at this account size. They compound credibility, not reach.
- Posting more will not do it. There is no volume of 3,550-follower posts that reaches
  6,450 new people reliably.
- **Only algorithmic distribution does it**, which means short-form video, which means the
  clips ([`plan.md`](plan.md) §1.1) — and X's job changes accordingly.

> **Short-form is the growth engine. X is the credibility engine.**
> Clips make people aware of you. X is where they decide you're worth following.

Plan X as the *conversion* surface, not the acquisition surface, and it stops being
disappointing.

## 1. The time budget, which is the binding constraint

Live 7 PM–3 AM. Sleep 3 AM–11 AM. That leaves **11 AM–7 PM for everything**: show prep,
clipping, building, X, and a life.

| Window | Minutes | What |
|---|---|---|
| **11:30 AM** | **30** | Reply block one — Tier A, first-ten-minute hits |
| **2:00 PM** | **20** | One original + the daily Overnight graphic |
| **5:30 PM** | **20** | Reply block two + tomorrow's tease |
| Rolling | — | **Clips are a production task**, budgeted against the show, not against X |

**70 minutes a day. Not more.** Beyond that the show suffers, and the show is the moat. An
X plan that quietly eats four hours is how the streak breaks in week five.

Two rules that protect the budget:

- **Notifications on for Tier A only.** Everything else is checked inside the blocks or not
  at all.
- **No X during the show.** The chat is on the broadcast; the account can wait.

## 2. The account as a product

Before volume, fix the thing volume points at. Someone arrives from a clip and spends four
seconds deciding.

- **The handle is not the brand — the bio is.** One line that says what you do and one that
  proves it. *"I run a 24/7 crypto TV network. Live 7 PM–3 AM ET. Anyone can claim an hour."*
  Concrete, checkable, and it contains an offer.
- **The pinned post is a demo, not an announcement.** A clip with the ticker running —
  something only this codebase could produce. Never pin a milestone.
- **The header is the schedule.** Literally: tonight's board. It changes, which is itself a
  signal that something is running.
- **Every profile visitor should hit one artifact that proves the network exists** within
  one scroll: a Book graphic, a Called It card, a lower third of somebody else's call.

Re-cut the bio and pinned at day 30 and day 60 against what actually converted.

## 3. The tiers — and how to build them without inventing them

I can verify exactly three accounts relevant to this plan: **@blknoiz06** (Ansem),
**@LaunchOnSF** (SF / StonkFun), and **@CSGNet** (yours). Everything else you build
yourself, with a procedure, because a list of handles someone else guessed at is a list of
mistakes.

### Tier A — 15 accounts, checked daily

The accounts where a good reply is worth more than one of your posts.

**How to build it:** for the next two weeks, every time a post reaches you organically,
record the author. At the end, rank by *how often their replies section contains people you
recognise* — that is the proxy for reply reach — and keep 15. **Re-cut monthly**, because
attention moves and a stale Tier A is the most common way this decays.

Rules: notifications on, first ten minutes or skip, never more than two replies a day into
the same account.

### Tier B — ~40 category peers

Crypto streamers, SocialFi builders, launchpad and terminal accounts.

**You are already building this list.** The CSGN Set from
[`design/csgn-share.md`](design/csgn-share.md) §2.1 is a published roster of ~120 channels
in exactly this category, with published inclusion criteria. **The ratings book is the
follow list.** Follow every Set member; reply into the ones who post.

### Tier C — the fifty you're recruiting

200–2,000-follower streamers with real opinions and no room. **This is the only tier where
you initiate**, and it is the tier that actually matters, because Charter contributors are
the product ([`design/the-grid.md`](design/the-grid.md) §5).

**How to find them:** Twitch and Kick directory, crypto categories, sorted ascending by
viewers. Anyone live to fewer than 30 people with a real take is a candidate. Follow, watch
ten minutes, reply to something specific, then offer an hour.

### Tier D — ten media accounts

Newsletters, aggregators, podcasters. You do not reply to them. You **feed them the Book**
once a month and let them quote it.

## 4. The reply doctrine

Replies are 60% of output and close to 100% of early reach.

**The rules:**

1. **First ten minutes or don't bother.** A reply at hour two is a diary entry.
2. **Never "great post."** Add a number, a counter-example, a correction, or a graphic. If
   you have none of those, keep scrolling — that is a successful use of the reply block.
3. **Never pitch.** Not in Tier A, not ever. The offer happens because someone clicked your
   profile, not because you put it in their replies.
4. **Reply to your own replies.** A second layer with the detail is where the follows come
   from.
5. **Quote-tweet yourself with the artifact** when a take lands, once, the next morning.

### 4.1 The Scoreboard Reply — the signature move

Somebody makes a call. It comes back as a **CSGN lower third**, rendered, with their handle
on it.

This is the highest-leverage habit in the entire plan and it should be a daily ritual:

- **Nobody else in that thread can produce it.** It is unfakeable without this codebase.
- It is a **gift**, not a pitch. People screenshot their own scoreboard.
- It advertises the network's actual capability instead of describing it.
- It compounds into the Caller Standings, which is the mindshare asset the archived strategy
  docs have been pointing at for months.

**One a day, minimum. It is the single thing on this list that would be a mistake to skip.**

## 5. The six post formats

Everything you post is one of these. If it isn't, don't.

**1. THE OVERNIGHT** — daily, ~9 AM, one graphic, one number.
> `Last night's Prime share: 4.2 ▲ 0.8 vs. 4-week.`
No guest, no idea, no energy required, and it compounds into an archive. This is the post
that runs on the days you have nothing.

**2. THE OPEN SLOT** — the inventory as the creative.
> `4 PM ET. Nobody's claimed it. You're on.`
Free, ninety seconds, can run every day for five years, and it is literally the product. The
day every hour is taken, run *that* instead: the full board, and *"Full. Come back tomorrow
at 3 AM."*

**3. THE RECEIPT** — a specific post-mortem.
> `Fixed Phantom connect today. Root cause: a cached address isn't a connection — we skipped connect() and the signature prompt never fired.`
Engineers respect specificity and almost nobody in crypto publishes failures. This is how
you get known among builders, which is how you get taken seriously by funds.

**4. THE SCOREBOARD REPLY** — §4.1. Daily.

**5. THE THESIS** — one a week, one idea, no thread padding.
> `Social apps tokenised who you know. CSGN tokenises when you're watched.`

**6. THE CHARTER COUNTDOWN** — the arc.
> `37 / 50. Thirteen more and this is a network.`

**Standing rules:** never post "we're live" without a reason to click. Never thread past
seven. Every post points at a schedule, not a vibe. Announcements are 5% of output — yes,
five.

- **9 AM** — the Overnight, scheduled the night before. It lands while the US is waking up

- **5:30 PM** — reply block two, plus the night's tease.

exists to move.

## 8. Kill criteria

Pre-committed, checked at day 30 and day 60.

- **Under 60 replies in a week** → the reply block isn't happening. Move it *before* the
  show, not after.
- **No Scoreboard Reply for a week** → the highest-leverage habit has lapsed. It goes back
  in before anything else on this list.
- **Follower growth flat two weeks running while clips ship** → the problem is the
  *account*, not the volume. Rewrite bio and pinned; cut originals in favour of replies.
- **Under 6,000 at day 60** → short-form is under-resourced. [`plan.md`](plan.md) §5 already
  commits to tripling clip output and cutting X originals to the minimum. Do it, don't
  debate it.
- **The show slips because of X** → cut X to the 11:30 block only. The show is the moat;
  the account is the funnel. Never trade the moat for the funnel.

## 9. What this account will not do

- **No engagement pods, no reply automation, no bought followers.** The number would move
  and nothing real would.
- **No pitching in other people's rooms or replies.** Be useful for weeks; the DM happens
  after, or not at all.
- **No announcing mechanisms before they work.** Ship, then show.
- **No arguing.** Ratio-bait is reach you can't spend and a reputation you can't return.
