### 1.1 August is the opening

The instinct that this is a bad month to start a sports channel is backwards.

**August is the deadest month in American sports, and that is the opportunity.** The
take-competition is at its weakest all year, preview appetite is at its highest, and millions
of people are actively looking for opinions because they are drafting fantasy teams in
nine days. A new account trying to break through in November is shouting over everyone. A new
account in August is one of the few people talking.

**Goal: the streak starts and the reel rhythm becomes a habit.** Nothing else this week
matters as much as those two things.

**Gate:** 21 reels shipped, streak unbroken, showbar working.

### Week 2 · Aug 17–23 — Own the poll

**The AP Top 25 drops at noon ET on Monday. This is the week the channel gets noticed.**

- **`TOP25` board shipped by Monday morning.** Hard deadline.
- **Monday is a rankings day, all day.** React live at noon. A full teardown in THE OPEN.
  Every reel that day is a ranking reel — `TIER LIST` on the top 25, `THE RECEIPT` on the
  worst preseason takes, `THE NUMBER` on the most overrated team.
- First interviews Tue/Wed/Thu.
- Preseason Wk 2 Aug 20.
- Start the `CALL IT` record publicly — preseason predictions, logged, with your name on them.

**Gate:** the Top 25 board on air, one reel past 25k views, first guest clip sent within 24h.

### Week 3 · Aug 24–30 — Draft season, then football

**The busiest week. Fantasy peaks and football arrives.**

- **Fantasy draft content daily** — tiers, sleepers, busts, draft-day rules. This is the
  highest-intent search window of the entire year for this audience, and tier lists are the
  most-shared sports format there is.
- Preseason Wk 3 Aug 27–29 + roster cuts.
- **Aug 29: CFB WEEK 0.** First real game-day broadcast. `MATCHUP` cards shipped by now.
  Ticker: rank chips, possession fix, section-dot clamp, item cap — all before this date.
- Aug 30: US Open main draw begins; it fills the 12–2 AM hours for two weeks.

**Gate:** `MATCHUP` on air, Week 0 broadcast ran without dead air, ticker survived a 60-game
Saturday.

### Week 4 · Aug 31 – Sep 5 — Season open

- 53-man cuts → reaction content.
- **Thu Sep 3: CFB Week 1 opens.** Thursday-night football, live.
- **Sat Sep 5: the big Saturday.** Full game-day. This is the day the last three weeks were

- Sunday Sep 6: Notre Dame–Wisconsin at Lambeau.

**Gate:** the Saturday broadcast is the best thing the channel has produced. Ship the Situation
Strip if it's ready; don't ship it that morning if it isn't.

## 3. The reel engine

Three a day, every day. **All three cut from the broadcast**, at the clip beats in
[`shows.md`](shows.md) §1.1 — 7:20, 11:15, 1:50. No separate shoot, ever.

| Slot | Format | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **~9 AM** | **THE TAKE** | One opinion on the day's biggest story, while the story is live |
| **~1 PM** | **THE RANKING** | A list or tier. **The most-shared sports format** — people comment to disagree with placements they don't even care about |
| **~6 PM** | **THE RECEIPT** | A bad take answered with data. **This is the information battle, run daily** |

### 3.1 Rules that decide whether a reel works

1. **The take is the first sentence.** Not the setup. You have 1.5 seconds.
2. **Burn the captions in.** Most of this is watched muted.
3. **One idea per reel.** Two ideas is two reels on two days.
4. **The last line is the caption.** Write it once, use it twice.
5. **Post native to each platform.** Same cut, three uploads — never a link.
6. **Reply to the first ten comments.** On TikTok and Reels this is a distribution lever,
   not a courtesy.

### 3.2 Cross-posting

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X. Same vertical file. **X clips also serve the
Scoreboard Reply habit** in [`x-playbook.md`](x-playbook.md) §4.1 — a rendered CSGN graphic in
someone else's replies is unfakeable without this codebase, and it is the single
highest-leverage daily action in the growth plan.

## 6. Kill criteria

Beyond those already committed in [`plan.md`](plan.md) §5:

- **Reels under 3/day for a week** → the clip beats aren't being marked in the rundown. Fix
  the show structure, not the editing.
- **The Top 25 board isn't ready by Aug 17 noon** → run the segment anyway with a plain
  `TAKE` card. **Never delay a tentpole for a graphic.**
- **Week 0 broadcast has dead air** → cut the block to 7 PM–12 AM for Week 1 and rebuild.
  A short clean show beats a long broken one, and Sep 5 is the one that matters.
- **Fantasy content underperforms all of Week 3** → the audience isn't fantasy-shaped. Drop
  it, go all-in on CFB previews, and don't spend Week 4 relitigating.
