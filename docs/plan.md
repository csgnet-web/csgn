## 1. The two calls this plan is built on

### 1.1 Short-form is the growth engine. X is the credibility engine.

Every previous plan in this repository made X the primary surface and rated TikTok/Shorts
a second-tier "also do this." At 3,550 followers, replies compound *credibility* but they
do not produce 72 net new followers a day. Only algorithmic distribution does that, and
CSGN is sitting on the raw material for it: **hours of live video produced daily, of which
zero is currently cut into verticals.**

**TikTok / Reels / Shorts move to P0.** Three clips a day rising to five — roughly 350
over the quarter. X keeps doing the job it is actually good at: replies into big accounts,
build receipts, and the thesis posts that make someone take the project seriously once
short-form has made them aware of it.

**The content engine is one input, seven outputs**, and the show has to be designed so
this falls out of it rather than being post-production: every broadcast hour yields
1 VOD → 3–5 verticals → 2 X posts → 1 Farcaster post → 1 Discord drop → 1 ticker headline
→ 1 Substack paragraph.

### 1.2 The Charter 50 is the story

A campaign needs an arc. The arc is a countdown: **50 contributors and the network
declares itself.** ([`design/the-grid.md`](design/the-grid.md) §5.)

It starts at zero on day 1, in public, on air. Every post between now and the fiftieth has
a reason to exist, every new contributor is a small event, and crossing the line is a
genuine one. It converts the project's weakest fact — nobody is making content for it yet
— into its most compelling artifact, and it costs nothing but a badge and a name read out
on air.

## 2. The three phases

### Days 1–30 — Fill the grid

**The one thing that matters this month: the nightly hour, never missed, and three clips a
day.** Everything else is second.

- **Ship Grid accrual, read-only.** Every holder sees their seconds. No uploads, no
  playout, no risk — and it is the daily notification that makes holding feel like a job.
- **Open submissions by link** + the review queue ([`design/the-grid.md`](design/the-grid.md) §6.2).
- **The Charter counter live** on air, on `/watch`, in the bio, in every post.
- **Turn on the ratings poller.** It ships dark and collects. *Every day it is off is a
  day of history that cannot be recovered* — [`design/csgn-share.md`](design/csgn-share.md) §7.
- **The Overnight post**, daily, ~9 AM ET: one graphic, yesterday's number. No guest, no
  idea required, and it compounds into an archive.
- Substack essays 1–2. 15 replies/day. 3 clips/day. Daily presence in the room.

**Day 30 gate:** 15+ Charter contributors, 90 clips published, the streak unbroken.

### Days 31–60 — Publish the receipts

The month the project stops describing itself and starts showing its homework.

- **The Grid on air**, Overnight daypart only, then wider if it holds.
- **First revenue post and first Airtime Buyback report**
  ([`design/token-economics.md`](design/token-economics.md) §5.1–5.2). Publish it even
  when the number is small — *especially* when it is small. A buyback policy that only
  starts reporting once the figures flatter us is one nobody will believe later.
- **The first Book**, with competitors' rows in it
  ([`design/csgn-share.md`](design/csgn-share.md) §5). This is the moment CSGN stops being
  a participant in the category and becomes the body that keeps its record. The day a
  competitor screenshots their own row to brag, the strategy has worked.
- **A generic Partner Day**, as the rehearsal — prove the toggle on a low-stakes tenant
  before it ever carries a name that matters.
- Clips to 5/day. Substack 3–4. First venue pitched and filmed.

**Day 60 gate:** the four kill criteria in §5 checked honestly, out loud, in writing.

### Days 61–90 — Become the name

- **The Charter 50 declaration.** On air, as an event. The essay, the clip, the graphic,
  the fifty names.
- **$ANSEM Day built, configured, dark, and demoed.** Not a pitch — a working takeover of
  a real television network, shown to the person whose token it would run on, with one
  boolean between it and live ([`design/the-grid.md`](design/the-grid.md) §8.3). If the
  answer is yes, it is a tentpole. If it is no, the surface stays and the next tenant
  fills it.
- **Publish the schema and the hooks** so other people can build frontends. Farcaster's
  lesson is that 45 clients beat one app; other people's frontends are free distribution.
- **Open-source one module** with a technical write-up — the `/player` state machine or
  the fee-tier engine. Developer credibility is sitting unclaimed in code already written.
- Substack 5. The Book monthly from here, forever.

## 3. The weekly cadence

Designed for the version of the founder who doesn't feel like it. Roughly 90 minutes a day
outside the broadcast.

| Every day | Every week |
|---|---|
| 15 replies into 10k+ accounts | 1 Substack essay, same day each week |
| **3–5 verticals to TikTok / IG / Shorts** | 1 Book or standings graphic |
| 1 Overnight graphic | 1 build-receipt thread |
| 1 build receipt or live artifact | 1 partner or creator DM |
| The nightly hour | 1 Charter-50 progress post |
| 1 hour in the room — useful, never pitching | Review the numbers in §5 |

**@CSGNet's job is the scoreboard, not announcements.** Today's grid, who produced it,
what it did. The rule from the archived pitch still stands and is the best rule in this
repository: **the empty slot is the creative.** *"4 PM ET. Nobody's claimed it. You're
on."*

**Never** post "we're live" without a reason to click. **Never** thread past 7 posts.
**Never** pitch in someone else's room.

## 4. Where the breakouts come from

Follower targets do not arrive from cadence alone. Name the candidate breakout moments in
advance so they get *produced* rather than hoped for:

1. **The Charter 50 declaration** — the arc paying off, around day 60–75.
2. **The first Book containing a competitor's row.** Rankings get quoted, argued with, and
   screenshotted by the people in them. Nothing else in the plan has that property.
3. **An $ANSEM Day**, if it happens — a partner's whole community arriving at once.
4. **The venue clip.** A bar full of people watching a crypto channel is a physical fact
   that no competitor can fake from a laptop.
5. **The Scoreboard Reply as a daily habit.** Somebody makes a call; it comes back as a
   CSGN lower-third in the replies. Nobody else in that thread can produce it, and it is
   unfakeable without this codebase. Highest-leverage single habit in the document.

## 5. Kill criteria — decided now, while nobody is emotional

Checked at day 30 and day 60, in writing.

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Clips < 3/day for two consecutive weeks | The pipeline is broken. **Fix it before anything else in this document.** It is the growth engine |
| Charter contributors < 15 at day 30 | **The offer is wrong, not the marketing.** Pay the first fifty from the treasury and say so |
| Grid fill < 20% of the 16 hours at day 60 | Community programming has failed. Fall back to curated house VOD, publicly, and stop calling it a network |
| @CSGNet < 1,500 at day 60 | Stop announcing entirely. The account becomes purely the scoreboard |
| Founder < 6,000 at day 60 | Short-form is under-resourced. Triple clip output, cut X originals to the minimum |
| The nightly streak breaks 3× in a month | **Cut the show's length, never the streak.** The streak is the asset |
| Rate card published and unfilled for a full quarter | The falsification test in [`design/token-economics.md`](design/token-economics.md) §8 has fired. Say so out loud |

## 6. What this plan refuses

- **No airdrop, no points program, no farming.** Every one buys a crowd that leaves the
  day it ends.
- **No "utility" language.** Nobody has ever wanted utility. They want a slot, a vote, and
  a scoreboard.
- **No buying followers, no engagement pods, no reply-guy automation.** The metric would
  move and nothing real would.
- **No pitching in other people's rooms.** Be useful for weeks; the ask happens in DMs or
  not at all.
- **No forwarding anyone's stream without explicit permission.** This is the one mistake
  that ends a relationship permanently and publicly.
- **No announcing a mechanism before it works.** Ship, then show.
